Admin controls

Manage account approvals, each user's daily AI response limit, and safely remove unwanted accounts. A limit of 2 and a limit of 20 can be assigned to different users. Limits are enforced by the backend, not just the counter shown on screen.

Limits accept whole numbers from 0 to 1000 per day. A limit of 0 blocks regular Geini chat replies. The Remove button permanently deletes a non-admin account and revokes its sessions. The usage counter resets at midnight Asia/Kolkata. Animation-observation calls do not consume the daily chat quota.
